# Land Degradation, Pollution, Deforestation, and Biodiversity

This excerpt summarizes publicly available themes from the IPBES Assessment Report on Land Degradation and Restoration. It is a retrieval aid and does not replace the full assessment.

## Interacting drivers

IPBES assesses how land degradation interacts with biodiversity loss. Deforestation, habitat fragmentation, pollution, and unsustainable agricultural intensification are among the drivers that reduce habitat quality and species persistence.

Pollution pressure is linked to reduced habitat quality and can impair sensitive taxa. Reducing pollutant pathways through buffers, input reduction, and containment is consistent with degradation-response framing.

## Restoration options

IPBES discusses restoration and sustainable land management options. Restoring degraded lands, reconnecting fragmented habitats, and improving soil and vegetation condition are presented as pathways to recover ecosystem functions. Multi-metric approaches that address soil, vegetation, and landscape structure together are more aligned with assessment findings than single-variable fixes.

## Uncertainty and context

IPBES emphasizes that effectiveness of interventions depends on ecological and social context. This knowledge base therefore avoids inventing quantitative recovery percentages for any single practice.
